Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026 · Beta version

FocusLab (“we”) provides short cognitive training exercises. This policy describes information handled by the FocusLab beta app and its service.

Information we handle

  • Guest account: when you continue, the app creates a random account identifier and authentication token. The server stores a one-way hash of the token, your selected training preferences, app language and consent time. FocusLab does not require your name, email address or password to use the beta.
  • Training activity: exercise sessions, answers, timing, scores and progress are stored to provide adaptive sessions and reports.
  • Support messages: if you contact support, we receive the details and contact information you choose to include.
  • Device and network data: our hosting provider may process connection data, such as IP address and request logs, to deliver and secure the service.

How we use information

We use this information to operate the app, save progress, adapt exercise difficulty, provide reports, troubleshoot issues and protect the service. We do not sell personal information or use training data for advertising profiles.

Advertising and analytics

The beta build is configured without production advertising unit IDs, so it does not display production ads. The app includes Google Mobile Ads SDK and a consent flow; if advertising is enabled in a later release, we will update this policy and applicable consent disclosures before serving ads. FocusLab does not currently use a separate product analytics service.

Storage and service providers

Account and training records are stored in the service database hosted by Railway. Data is transmitted between the app and service over HTTPS. We may use infrastructure providers to host and secure the service; they process information on our behalf.

Retention and deletion

We retain guest account and training records while the account is active. You can delete your account in the app’s Profile settings; deletion removes the account and associated records from the service database. Operational backups or security logs may persist for a limited period as required to protect and operate the service.

Your choices

You can change training preferences and language in the app, export your training data from Profile, or delete your account from Profile. You can stop using the beta at any time.

Children

FocusLab is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the beta changes. The current version will be posted here. For privacy questions, contact noivuduc@gmail.com.
